02Discrimination

Calibration asks whether confidence is honest on average; discrimination asks whether it ranks. A threshold buys accuracy only if a confident answer is likelier to be right than an unconfident one. Automate the most confident answers first and read the error rate among them: a curve that falls to the left means escalating the least confident answers removes errors, and a flat one means it removes volume and nothing else.
